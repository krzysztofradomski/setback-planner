Footprints are measured bounding envelopes, including entrance recesses. They exclude carports, terraces and roof overhangs. No dimensions were inferred from promotional renders. DE75 plan explicitly states building footprint area 92.20 m² and usable area 75.60 m²; DE165 selected plan states total area 164.76 m². Its alternate public plans `_05` and `_06` label 167.11 and 167.57 m² with the same outer dimensions.

The pictures establish gabled roof appearance. Verified numeric wall/ridge heights, pitch and overhang dimensions were not found in the public gallery or technical documents (`docs/naszatechnologia.pdf`, `docs/tech_2.pdf`). Editor defaults are illustrative and must not be presented as manufacturer measurements. Ground-floor plans do not establish opening-free elevations: e.g. DE135's second rendering includes an upper gable opening.
